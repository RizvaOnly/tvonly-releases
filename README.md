# tvOnly — releases

Downloads for **tvOnly**, an IPTV player for Android TV / Google TV.

**Install with Downloader:** open the Downloader app on your TV and enter the tvOnly code,
or this link:

    https://github.com/RizvaOnly/tvonly-releases/releases/latest/download/tvOnly.apk

The app updates itself: when a new version is published here, tvOnly offers it on launch.

tvOnly ships no channels, playlists or content of any kind. You add your own
M3U playlist, Xtream Codes or Stalker portal from your provider.
